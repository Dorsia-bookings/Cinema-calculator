Other obligations to be aware of
Superannuation: If you pay staff or pay yourself a wage, 11.5% super applies on top (2024–25 rate).
Payroll tax (Tasmania): Only applies if your total wages exceed $1.25M/year — unlikely at this scale.
Liquor licence: Annual fee varies by licence type in Tasmania. Budget $500–$2,000/year. Apply through CBOS.
BAS lodgement: Quarterly. Register for GST once turnover exceeds $75,000/year — at these revenue levels you will exceed that threshold.
PAYG instalments: Once profitable, the ATO will require quarterly income tax prepayments. Factor this into cash flow.
